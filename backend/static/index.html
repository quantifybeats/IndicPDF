FAQ

Common questions

APS and Anu Telugu are legacy 8-bit font encodings developed before Unicode was widely adopted in India. When documents made with these fonts are exported to PDF, the encoding is not preserved — the PDF renderer displays replacement boxes (□) instead of Telugu characters. IndicPDF AI includes a complete remapping table for both font families and converts them to proper Unicode before rendering.

Uploaded files and converted PDFs are automatically deleted within 2 hours. A scheduled deletion worker runs every 30 minutes. We never read, index, or analyze the content of your documents. Storage is encrypted at rest using AES-256, and all transfers use TLS 1.3.

After conversion, PaddleOCR-VL re-reads the output PDF and extracts visible text. We compare this against the original DOCX source using Levenshtein distance similarity. A score of 95%+ means the rendered PDF text is nearly identical to the source — a clean, corruption-free conversion. Scores below 85% trigger a warning and detailed corruption report.

Our searchability verification step confirms the text layer is intact before your file is made available for download.

The Print-Ready mode outputs PDFs compatible with major print-on-demand platforms including CMYK color space, bleed marks, and embedded fonts. KDP-specific profiles are available in the Pro plan. Notion Press and IngramSpark compatibility is in active testing.
